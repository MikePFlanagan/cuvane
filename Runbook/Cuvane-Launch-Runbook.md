## Step 1 — Buy the Domains (non-negotiable)

Do this **first**. The name is the asset; every step after depends on owning it.

1. Go to **Porkbun** (cheapest for `.ai`, all TLDs in one account) or Namecheap.
2. Buy all three: `cuvane.com` (~$10), `cuvane.ai` (~$60–80), `cuvane.io` (~$40–50).
3. Enable **auto-renew** and **WHOIS privacy** (free at Porkbun).
4. Register them in your personal name now; transfer to the company after the
   legal entity exists (Step 6).

> Why all three? `.com` is trust and long-term value. `.ai` is your industry's
> TLD. `.io` is the developer/startup TLD. Owning the set keeps the name out of
> squatters' hands and gives every future product a home.

## Step 2 — Claim Developer Identity (free)

- **GitHub organization:** `github.com/organizations/new` → name **cuvane**.
- **npm scope:** `npmjs.com/settings/<you>/scopes` → create `@cuvane`.
- **Social handles:** grab `@cuvane` on X and `linkedin.com/company/cuvane`.

This is free and takes five minutes. Squatters take these daily — do not wait.

## Step 4 — Website (free)

Static sites are free to host and plenty for a parent company.

1. Build a one-page site on `cuvane.com` — tagline, the four products, contact.
   Plain HTML/CSS is enough (a template is ready to drop in).
2. Host on **GitHub Pages** or **Cloudflare Pages** (both free).
3. Point the domain's DNS (`CNAME` for the `.com`) at the host.
4. Add SSL — automatic and free on both platforms.

## Step 5 — Trademark (deferrable, self-filed)

You already own the name through first use (common law) once the site is live.
Federal registration makes it enforceable nationwide.

- File yourself at `tmsearch.uspto.gov` (Trademark Center), standard character
  mark, **CUVANE**.
- Start with **Class 42** (SaaS/AI services) — **$250** via TEAS Plus.
  Add Class 9 (software) and Class 35 (business services) later, same cost each.
- Specimen: screenshot of the Cuvane homepage with the logo beside a service
  description.

**Budget move:** if cash is tight this month, launch first and file the
trademark in the same month as your first revenue.

---

## Step 6 — Legal Entity (shoestring path)

You do **not** need a $500 Delaware C-Corp to start. Pick the cheapest path that
lets you operate legally and own the brand.

**Option A — Use your existing LLC (cheapest, $0)**
Operate the brand under your current entity (`MICHAELFLANAGANLLC`), register
**Cuvane** as a trademark/trade name of that LLC, and file the trademark in its
name. Rebrand/upgrade later.

**Option B — New LLC via Bizee (your proven route, ~$0–$99)**
Bizee (formerly Incfile) offers a free LLC formation tier — you pay only your
state's filing fee (roughly $40–$500 depending on state). First year of
registered agent service is included free at Bizee.

**Option C — Delaware C-Corp (later, ~$500+)**
Only when you raise outside capital or need investor-grade structure. Forming it
now is premature on a shoestring budget.

**Don't forget:** file the **BOIR** (Beneficial Ownership Information) at
`fincen.gov` — free, required for most new entities.
