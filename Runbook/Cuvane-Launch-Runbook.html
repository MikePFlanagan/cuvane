Step 1 — Buy the Domains

Non-negotiable. The name is the asset — do this first.

  • Go to Porkbun (cheapest for .ai, all TLDs in one account) or Namecheap.
  • Buy all three: cuvane.com ~$10, cuvane.ai ~$60–80, cuvane.io ~$40–50.
  • Enable auto-renew and WHOIS privacy (free at Porkbun).
  • Register in your personal name now; transfer to the company after the entity exists.

Why all three? .com is trust and long-term value. .ai is your industry’s TLD. .io is the developer/startup TLD. Owning the set keeps the name out of squatters’ hands.

Step 2 — Claim Developer Identity FREE

  • GitHub organization: github.com/organizations/new → name cuvane.
  • npm scope: npmjs.com/settings/<you>/scopes → create @cuvane.
  • Social handles: @cuvane on X and linkedin.com/company/cuvane.

Squatters take these daily. Do not wait.

Step 4 — Website FREE

  1. One-page site on cuvane.com — tagline, four products, contact. Plain HTML/CSS is enough.
  2. Host on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages — both free.
  3. Point DNS (CNAME for the .com) at the host.
  4. SSL is automatic and free on both platforms.

Step 5 — Trademark DEFERRABLE

First use gives you common-law rights once the site is live. Federal registration makes it enforceable nationwide.

  • File yourself at tmsearch.uspto.gov, standard character mark, CUVANE.
  • Start with Class 42 (SaaS/AI services) — $250 via TEAS Plus. Add Classes 9 and 35 later, same cost each.
  • Specimen: screenshot of the Cuvane homepage with the logo beside a service description.

Budget move: if cash is tight this month, launch first and file the trademark in the same month as your first revenue.

Step 6 — Legal Entity SHOESTRING

You do not need a $500 Delaware C-Corp to start.

Option A — Existing LLC $0

Operate the brand under your current entity, register Cuvane as its trade name, file the trademark in its name. Rebrand/upgrade later.

Option B — Bizee LLC ~$0–99

Bizee (formerly Incfile) offers a free formation tier — pay only your state filing fee. First year of registered agent is included.

Option C — Delaware C-Corp ~$500+ (later)

Only when you raise outside capital or need investor-grade structure. Premature on a shoestring budget.

Don’t forget: file the BOIR (Beneficial Ownership Information) at fincen.gov — free, required for most new entities.

Step 7 — Google Workspace WHEN READY

Once revenue covers it, move email to Google Workspace Business Starter ($6/user/mo): real Gmail + Drive on @cuvane.com, shared calendars across the ventures. The free Zoho setup keeps everything until then.
