The operating system for your everyday.

PersonalAI is a local-first personal intelligence and memory layer — the quiet layer that remembers, organizes, and runs your life. Yours. On your devices. Not in someone else's cloud.

What it is

Most software waits to be opened. PersonalAI doesn't work that way — it works underneath your day, holding context the way a memory should.

People, plans, projects, priorities. Decisions half-made in one app, finished in another. PersonalAI is the layer that keeps it all connected — without asking you to live inside one more dashboard.

It is being shaped on the Cuvane machine: raw attention in, clarity out.

Built on four pillars

Memory

A persistent layer that remembers context across apps, sessions, and days.

Context

Everything it surfaces is grounded in what actually happened — never guessed.

Orchestration

The quiet work of organizing the day, so you act instead of administrate.

Yours

Local-first by design. Your life stays on your devices, in your control.
